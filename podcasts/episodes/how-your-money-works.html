How Your Money Works in South Africa

Understand how money moves through South Africa's financial system, how banks operate and how interest rates influence everyday financial decisions.

About This Episode

How does money actually work in South Africa? Where does it come from, how does it move through the economy and what role do banks play?

In this episode, we explore South Africa's financial system, including the role of commercial banks, the South African Reserve Bank and the country's monetary policy.

We also examine how interest rates, inflation and financial institutions influence households, businesses and the broader economy.

What You'll Learn

  • What money is and how it works
  • How commercial banks operate
  • The role of the South African Reserve Bank
  • How interest rates influence borrowing and saving
  • What inflation means for everyday consumers
  • How money moves through the economy
