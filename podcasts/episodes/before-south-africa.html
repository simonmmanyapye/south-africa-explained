About This Episode

What existed in the region we now call South Africa before European colonial expansion?

In this episode, we explore the communities, societies and kingdoms that developed across southern Africa over many centuries.

We examine early human history, established communities, trade networks, agriculture, political organisation and the diverse ways people lived before colonial rule.

What You'll Learn

  • Early human history in southern Africa
  • Indigenous communities and societies
  • Early agriculture and settlement patterns
  • Trade networks and regional connections
  • The development of African kingdoms
  • Life in the region before European colonial expansion
