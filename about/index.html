ABOUT SOUTH AFRICA EXPLAINED

Understanding South Africa, One Story at a Time.

South Africa is a country shaped by complex systems, remarkable history, diverse cultures and constant change. South Africa Explained exists to make these subjects easier to understand through accessible and informative content.

WHO WE ARE

Making South Africa Easier to Understand

South Africa Explained is an educational and informational platform dedicated to exploring the country and the systems, events, people and ideas that shape it.

From politics and business to history, law, technology and lifestyle, we break down complicated subjects into clear, accessible explanations that help audiences understand how South Africa works.

Through articles, podcasts and other forms of digital content, our goal is to make knowledge easier to access and encourage curiosity, understanding and discovery.
